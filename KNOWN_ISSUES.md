# Known issues

- **iniBuilds A350:** in the cabin, the engine whine keeps playing, distorted, after the thrust comes back from takeoff to climb. In the flight itself it stops there.
- **iniBuilds A380:** in external views, from certain angles, the engine whine plays incorrectly (possibly related to a350 issue above)
- **iniBuilds A380 and A340, Fenix A320 family, and probably other fly-by-wire aircraft:** the ailerons, elevator and rudder don't follow the clip, because the aircraft's own fly-by-wire keeps moving them. On the A380 the elevator stays at full nose-up.
- **Working Title 787-10:** in takeoff replays the engines run about 6% N1 off the flight's on average. The first seconds, at idle and spooling up, are close; where in the takeoff roll or climb they part, and whether high or low, isn't known yet.
- **Working Title 747-8:** in takeoff replays the engines run about 4.5% N1 off the flight's on average, likely the same gap as on the 787-10.
